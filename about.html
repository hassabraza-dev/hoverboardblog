About Us

Welcome to the Hoverboards Blog, your number one source for all things hoverboards. We're dedicated to giving you the very best of hoverboard news, with a focus on reviews, safety, and the latest technology.

Founded in 2025, Hoverboards Blog has come a long way from its beginnings. When we first started out, our passion for self-balancing scooters drove us to start our own blog. We now serve customers all over the world, and are thrilled to be a part of the quirky, eco-friendly wing of the transportation industry.

We hope you enjoy our blog as much as we enjoy offering it to you. If you have any questions or comments, please don't hesitate to contact us.
